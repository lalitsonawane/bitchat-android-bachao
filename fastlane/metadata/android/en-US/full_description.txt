A decentralized peer-to-peer messaging app that works over Bluetooth mesh networks. bitchat lets you talk to anyone nearby even when the internet is blocked, overloaded, or unavailable.

No registration, no phone numbers, no tracking, no ads. Just install and start chatting. Free and open source.

Also join location-based geohash channels to talk with people in your neighborhood, city, province, or region. You can leave notes at a location for anyone who passes by. Stay connected anywhere, on your terms.

Bluetooth mesh chats work offline. Geohash channels use the internet via public Nostr relays and share only a coarse geohash—never precise GPS—with those relays.
